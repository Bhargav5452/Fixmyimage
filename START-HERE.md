# START HERE — FixMyImg

## What we are building

A free global image utility that makes images easier to upload to websites and forms.

The first tool:

> **Compress an image under a maximum KB/MB limit directly in the browser.**

## What makes the product different

Not just:

> "compress image"

The broader product goal is:

> **"Make my image fit the requirement."**

This allows future features for size, dimensions, format, crop, and validation.

## Why the MVP is cheap to operate

Images are processed in the user's browser. We do not need an image-processing server or AI API for the initial product.
